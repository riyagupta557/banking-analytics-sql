
# Business Recommendations

- **Promote digital payment services** through targeted campaigns and incentives to increase payment transaction adoption.

- **Strengthen overdue loan** monitoring with timely repayment reminders and proactive follow-ups.

- **Diversify lending across customer segments** to reduce concentration risk from **Large Enterprise borrowers**.

- **Regularly review high-exposure borrowers** to reassess lending limits and repayment capacity.

- **Conduct periodic audits of high-volume lending branches** to ensure loan growth is supported by strong credit quality.


# Business Insights

1. Large Enterprise customers hold the highest account balance (**₹28.61M**), making them the bank's most valuable deposit segment.

2. Every account holder has completed at least one transaction, reflecting strong customer engagement across the bank.

3. Business and Savings accounts contribute over **42%** of total deposits, making them the bank's primary funding sources.

4. Over **80%** of accounts are active, indicating a healthy customer base with low account attrition.

5. Monthly transaction values remained stable between **₹2.2M and ₹2.6M** during 2020–2023, showing consistent banking activity.

6. Deposits, Transfers, and Withdrawals dominate transaction activity, while Payment transactions have the lowest usage.

7. Although most loans are active, **10%** of the portfolio is overdue, highlighting existing credit risk.

8. Large Enterprise customers account for the highest loan exposure (**₹6.25M**), creating concentration risk within the loan portfolio.

9. A small group of customers holds multiple loans with high cumulative exposure, increasing portfolio risk if repayments weaken.

10. A few branches consistently issue the highest number of loans, making loan quality monitoring at these branches essential.
